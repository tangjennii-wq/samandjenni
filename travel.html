Getting here

Fly into JFK, LGA (closest — ~30 min) or EWR. From any of them, a taxi or Uber to Midtown runs roughly 30–60 minutes depending on traffic. Once you're here, the wedding, the welcome party and every hotel above are reachable by a short cab ride or the subway — you will not need a car.

The subway, briefly

  • Just tap your phone or card at the turnstile — that's OMNY, no MetroCard needed. $2.90 a ride, and it stops charging you after 12 rides in a week.
  • Express skips stops, local makes them all. Same colour, different letter or number — the 4 and 5 are express where the 6 is local. If your stop isn't on the express, take the local or you'll sail straight past it.
  • Check uptown vs downtown before you go down the stairs. At a lot of stations the two directions have separate entrances on opposite sides of the street, and there's no way to switch once you've tapped in.
  • Get Citymapper. It's better than Google Maps here — it knows which end of the train to stand at, and tells you when a line is a mess.
