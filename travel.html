Getting here

Getting here Fly into JFK, LGA (closest — ~30 min) or EWR. From any of them, a taxi or Uber to Midtown runs roughly 30–60 minutes depending on traffic. Once you're here, the wedding, the welcome party and every hotel above are reachable by a short cab ride or the subway — you will not need a car.

Questions

When should I book my hotel?
Now, honestly. These are courtesy blocks, which means rooms are held but not guaranteed forever — they're released back to the hotel closer to the date, and after that you'd pay the regular rate. Rooms are released back to the hotels on February 2, 2027.
What's the dress code?
Black tie, and colour is encouraged. There's a what to wear breakdown for every event on the events page.
Do I need to come Friday too?
We'd love you there. Friday is a welcome party at Chinese Tuxedo in Chinatown — a proper night, not a drop-in. If Friday shows up on your events page, you're on the list.
How do I RSVP?
Open the RSVP form — it takes a minute, and it only shows the events you’re invited to. Replies are due February 26, 2027.
