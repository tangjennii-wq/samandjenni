What will the weather be like?
Late March in New York is genuinely unpredictable — that's not a hedge, it's the season. Historically the back half of March runs about 13°C / 55°F in the afternoon and drops to 2–6°C / 36–43°F after dark, and it rains roughly one day in three. It has been 27°C on this date and it has snowed. Bring a coat you don't mind checking, and shoes that survive a wet pavement.
Which airport should I fly into?
LGA is closest — about 30 minutes to Midtown. JFK and EWR both work and often cost less; from any of the three a taxi or Uber runs 30–60 minutes depending on traffic. There's no need to hire a car: everything that weekend is a short cab ride or a subway stop apart, and parking in Midtown is its own kind of punishment.
How does the subway work?
Tap your phone or card straight at the turnstile — that's OMNY, no MetroCard needed, $2.90 a ride and it stops charging after 12 rides in a week. Two things catch visitors out: express trains skip stops (the 4 and 5 skip what the 6 makes), and uptown and downtown often have separate entrances on opposite sides of the street with no way to switch once you've tapped in. Full subway notes and the lines to each venue are on the hotels page.
Where should I stay?
We have blocks at Lotte New York Palace, Thompson Central Park and Le Méridien — our rate only comes through the booking link on each card. Beyond those there are 26 other hotels we'd happily send you to, filterable by neighbourhood, from citizenM Bowery at the cheap end to The Mercer at the other. See all of them.
